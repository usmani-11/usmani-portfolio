before you ask

Common questions

What do you work on?

Graphic design, logos, video editing for reels and promos, and websites. Many clients need a mix, which I handle in one place.

Do you take freelance work?

Yes. I'm open to freelance and part-time projects, from one-off graphics to complete websites.

Can you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. Everything is delivered digitally, and I'm comfortable working over WhatsApp and email.

How do I get started?

Send me a message with a few lines about your project using the form above. I'll reply with questions, a timeline and a quote.

What do I need to give you before we start?

Your business name, any existing logo or colors, a few examples of styles you like, and the text or photos you want included. If you don't have these yet, I'll help you figure them out.

Will I get the source files?

Yes. Once the project is finished and approved, I can share the final files in the formats you need, and a website can be published live for you.
