a little about me

One person, three crafts.

I'm Abubakar Usmani, based in Lahore, Pakistan. I started with graphic design, and slowly picked up video editing and web development because clients kept needing all three at once.

That's actually my biggest advantage: when I design your logo, I already know how it will look on your website and in your reels. Nothing gets lost between different people.

I'm currently studying CSIT at PITACH Government Institute, Lahore, and I take on freelance and part-time projects alongside it.
